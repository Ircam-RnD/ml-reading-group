| **#1** | **Self-Supervised Learning from Images with a Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (I-JEPA)** | Mahmoud Assran et al. | 2023 | arXiv:2301.08243 | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08243) | [I-JEPA GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/ijepa) | — | **Orian Sharoni & Luke Dzwonczyk** | **Core JEPA idea:** learn representations by predicting the latent representation of a target region from a context region, rather than reconstructing pixels. Foundation for non-generative self-supervised learning and later JEPA variants. |
| **#1** | **Music-JEPA: Learning a World Model of Sound from Action** | Ziyu Wang et al. | 2026 | arXiv:2607.22000 | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22000) | — | [Music-JEPA Demo](https://zzwaang.github.io/music-jepa-demo/) | **Orian Sharoni & Luke Dzwonczyk** | **JEPA applied to music/audio:** models music as an action-conditioned dynamical system. Audio is treated as the state and piano-roll information as the action, enabling a learned **world model of sound** and downstream tasks such as beat tracking, composer identification, key estimation, and piano transcription. |
